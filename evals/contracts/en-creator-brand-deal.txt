Creator Partnership Agreement

1. Deliverables
The Creator will publish one Instagram reel and two stories about the product.

2. Payment
The fee of USD 2,500 is payable within 14 days after publication.

3. Usage Rights
The Brand may use the content, the Creator's name and likeness in all media, worldwide and in perpetuity, including paid advertising, without further compensation.

4. Exclusivity
During the term and for twelve months afterwards, the Creator shall not work with any other brand in the beauty category.

5. Approval
The Creator shall make changes to the content until the Brand approves it.

6. Penalty
For each breach of this agreement the Creator shall pay a penalty of USD 5,000.

7. Indemnification
The Creator shall indemnify and hold the Brand harmless from all claims arising from the campaign, including claims caused by the Brand's own product.

8. Changes
The Brand may amend these terms at its sole discretion by notifying the Creator.
