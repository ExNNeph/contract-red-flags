Subcontractor Agreement

1. Services
The Subcontractor provides design services for the Agency's clients.

2. Payment
The Agency pays the Subcontractor only after the Agency has received payment from the end client for the respective project.

3. Revisions
The fee includes two rounds of revisions. Further changes are billed at the hourly rate.

4. Non-Compete
For 24 months after the end of this agreement, the Subcontractor shall not provide similar services to any company in Europe.

5. Credit
The Subcontractor waives any right to be named as the author of the designs.

6. Portfolio
The Subcontractor may not show the designs in a portfolio or mention the Agency's clients as references.

7. Term
This agreement renews automatically for successive twelve-month periods unless cancelled six months before the end of the term.

8. Confidentiality
The Subcontractor shall keep all information received from the Agency confidential indefinitely, whether or not it is public.

9. Liability
The Subcontractor is liable for all damages without limitation, including indirect damages and lost profits.
